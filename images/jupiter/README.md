# Exercise Jupiter images

Drop new images in this folder and tell Claude the filename — they get wired
into specific injects in `assets/js/scenario-jupiter.js`.

## Naming

    <subject>-sq.jpg      square, ~1000x1000, for Instagram
    <subject>-wide.jpg    16:9, ~1200x675, for X and Facebook

Same subject can have both. JPEG, under ~400KB each — participants may be on
mobile data in a car park.

## Gaps worth filling

The exercise runs 13:00–15:30 in September. Two of the three photos above were
taken at dusk in November, which does not match an afternoon incident. Daytime
images would be a real improvement, particularly:

- smoke rising above a rooftop, seen from a distance down a street
- fire appliances on a residential road, blue lights
- a taped-off road or car park entrance
- a group of staff standing out on grass away from a building

Distant and oblique shots work best — they carry the story without needing to
depict the real building accurately.
